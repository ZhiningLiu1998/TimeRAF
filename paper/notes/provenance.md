## Evidence policy for this draft

- Numerical claims are taken only from versioned or hash-bound artifacts.
- The first-page motivation figure is a controlled synthetic construction
  generated directly by `tools/generate_figures.py`; it makes no empirical
  claim. The real ETTh1 case remains in the method schematic and appendix.
- `data/real_retrieval_case.json` is generated by
  `tools/select_real_retrieval_case.py` from the saved ETTh1 TimeMixer
  validation and test prediction bundles. The script screens all
  origin--channel pairs with fixed visualization criteria, records the source
  hashes and top candidates, and recomputes the selected residual retriever.
  Its recomputed prediction must match the saved prediction within
  \(10^{-5}\). The selected case explains the mechanism and is not used for
  method selection or aggregate evidence.
- The 55-cell released-checkpoint summary is development evidence.
- The ETT official-test study is development evidence because ETTh2 diagnostics
  informed method design; the unused ETT post-test rows are confirmatory.

  `924d5e851f7f940d6ef7b7d3503c5281f1d744a41320254b36950f40684952de`.
  It contains 585 finite completed cells and passes the frozen primary gate.
